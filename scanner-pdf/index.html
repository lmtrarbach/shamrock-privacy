Privacy Policy — Shamrock Scanner to PDF

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Shamrock Scanner to PDF (“the app”) is developed by Luthiano Trarbach (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. Everything you scan stays on your device.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: when you tap Take photo, the app opens your device's camera app. The photo is handed back to the app only, and only for the page you are scanning.
  • Gallery: images are chosen through Android's system photo picker. The app only receives the images you select.
  • Copies of these images are kept in the app's private storage while you edit a scan, and deleted when you save or discard it.

Your documents

PDFs are saved only in your device's shared storage (Documents/Shamrock Scanner). We never upload them. They leave your device only if you choose to share them or open them with another app.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions of its own. The Google Play Billing library adds the billing and network-access permissions it needs to process the optional purchase through Google Play; the app itself sends nothing over the network.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data collection

We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not collect any personal or device data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
