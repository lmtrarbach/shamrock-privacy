Privacy Policy — Shamrock Screen Recorder

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Shamrock Screen Recorder (“the app”) is developed by Luthiano Trarbach (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data. Everything you record stays on your device.

Permissions and what they are used for

PermissionPurpose
Screen capture (MediaProjection)Recording your screen and taking screenshots. It is only active after you start a capture and confirm the system prompt.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Recording your voice and/or app audio into your screen recordings, only while a recording is in progress and only if you choose an audio source.
NotificationsShowing recording controls and “saved” notifications.
Display over other appsShowing the optional floating recording controls.
Foreground serviceKeeping the recording running while you use other apps.
Billing and network accessAdded by the Google Play Billing library to process the optional purchase through Google Play. The app itself sends nothing over the network.

Your recordings

Recordings and screenshots are saved only in your device's shared storage (Movies/Shamrock and Pictures/Shamrock). We never upload them. They leave your device only if you choose to share them with another app.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data collection

We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not collect any personal or device data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
