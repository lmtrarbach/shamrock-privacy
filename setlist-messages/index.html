Privacy Policy — Setlist Messages

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Setlist Messages ("the app") is developed by Luthiano Trarbach ("we"). It turns a message you write into a playlist whose song titles spell it out. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

We do not run servers, and we do not collect, sell or keep your data. To find songs and build your playlist, the app sends the words of your message to the music and AI services listed below. Everything else stays on your device.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Your message, to find songs. The words you type are sent as search terms to Apple's public iTunes Search API. When you save a playlist, they are sent to the music service you chose (Spotify or Apple Music) together with the matching song and artist names.
  • Your message, to the AI (optional). When AI song suggestions is on (Settings → AI), your message is sent to Pollinations (pollinations.ai), a free public AI service, which suggests matching song titles. Turn the setting off to stop this; the app then uses catalog search only.
  • Album artwork is downloaded from Apple's and Spotify's image servers.

Like any internet request, these services see your device's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply:

Apple, Spotify, and Pollinations (pollinations.ai).

Your music account

  • You sign in on Spotify's or Apple's own page; the app never sees your password.
  • The app receives access tokens that let it search the catalog and create playlists in your account (Spotify permissions: playlist-modify-private, playlist-modify-public). It does not read your listening history, library or profile details.
  • The tokens are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are excluded from backups. Settings → Disconnect deletes them. You can also revoke access at any time in your Spotify account (Apps) or Apple ID settings.

Your playlists

  • Playlists are created private in your music account.
  • When you tap Share on a Spotify playlist, the app makes that playlist public so the link opens for the people you send it to. You can make it private again in Spotify.
  • The setlists you generate and the playlists you create (message, song titles, artists and artwork links) are kept on your device so you can find, save or share them later. It is included in your Android device backup if you have backups turned on. Removing an entry from this list does not delete a saved playlist from your music account.

Premium preferences and purchases

  • Your favorite music styles and artists are stored on your device. The artist names are sent to Apple's iTunes Search API to find their songs, and to Pollinations when AI suggestions are on.
  • The optional Premium upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app uses the Internet permission, plus the billing permission the Google Play Billing library adds for the optional purchase. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Data collection

We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and we do not receive any of the data described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
